Three ways to keep it being read

Federal law has set September 17–23 apart as Constitution Week since 1956, and has required federally funded schools to teach the Constitution every September 17 since 2004. Nobody is asked to read it out loud.

1 Let Congress read it on the House and Senate floor

Ask your Representative and Senators to read the Constitution aloud during Constitution Week, and to make it a standing rule of their chamber.

(202) 224-3121U.S. Capitol switchboard
2 Sign the petition for an executive action

Making a reading the duty of every federal department, bureau, office and agency — starting in the Oval Office.

change.org/readconstitutionaloud
3 Introduce and support the bill

The Public Readings of the Founding Documents Act, and its companion the Commemorative Days Observance Act. Both are drafted and in the public domain.

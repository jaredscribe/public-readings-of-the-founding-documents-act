Printable handbills

Cards and posters for public readings of the founding documents. They are in the public domain: print them, hand them out, and change the date and the place for your own reading. No permission is needed.

Printing them

Print at actual size, 100 % — never “fit to page”, which shrinks the QR codes and the margins. Cards are double-sided: flip on the long edge. Nothing runs to the edge of the paper, so no bleed is needed, and a poster does not need cardstock.

A copy shop charges by the printed side, so the sheets that hold four cards cost about a quarter of a card printed on its own. With a week of lead time, an online postcard printer is cheaper still.

The sources

Each handbill is HTML and CSS rendered to PDF; the sources sit beside the PDFs, and each folder has a README with the print settings. Everything is also in the public repository with the model legislation, under CC0. The typefaces are EB Garamond and IM Fell English SC, under the SIL Open Font License, whose text ships beside them.

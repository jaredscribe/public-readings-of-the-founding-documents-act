Then ask Washington to read it too

Since 2004, federal law has required schools that receive federal funds to teach the Constitution every September 17. We ask Congress and the executive branch to do the same — out loud.

1 Write your Representative and Senators

Ask them to read the Constitution aloud on the floor every September 17, and to make it a standing rule of their chamber.

(202) 224-3121U.S. Capitol switchboard
2 Sign the petition

Ask the President to direct every federal agency to read the Constitution aloud each Constitution Day.

The Public Readings of the Founding Documents Act is drafted and in the public domain. Any member may introduce it.

# Handbills

Print-ready handouts for public readings, free to copy, print and hand out.

## Constitution Day, September 17, 2026: reading on the U.S. Capitol steps

Announces a reading of the Constitution on the steps of the U.S. Capitol at
11 a.m., Thursday, September 17, 2026, and asks readers to write their
Representative and Senators, sign the petition, and read the model bill in this
repository.

| File | What it is | How to print |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`2026-09-17-constitution-day-card-4x6.pdf`](2026-09-17-constitution-day-card-4x6.pdf) | Two-sided 4 × 6 in card: page 1 front, page 2 back | Duplex, **flip on long edge**, on 4 × 6 cardstock |
| [`2026-09-17-constitution-day-cards-2up-letter.pdf`](2026-09-17-constitution-day-cards-2up-letter.pdf) | The same card, two per letter sheet, with crop marks | Letter cardstock, duplex, flip on long edge, then cut on the marks |
| [`2026-09-17-constitution-day-poster-letter.pdf`](2026-09-17-constitution-day-poster-letter.pdf) | One-sided 8.5 × 11 in poster for bulletin boards and walls | Letter, single-sided |

Print at **Actual size / 100 %**, not "fit to page", which shrinks the QR
codes. Everything sits inside a white margin, so no bleed is needed.

The QR codes lead to:

- **The site:** <https://declareindependence.net/legislation-advocacy>
- **The petition:** <https://www.change.org/readconstitutionaloud>
- **This repository:** <https://github.com/jaredscribe/public-readings-of-the-founding-documents-act>

Each code was decoded from a 300 dpi render of the PDF and checked against those
addresses.

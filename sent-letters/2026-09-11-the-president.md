The President  
The White House  
1600 Pennsylvania Avenue NW  
Washington, DC 20500

Mr. President Donald Trump,

Constitution Day is Thursday, September 17. This year's Constitution Week proclamation
has not yet been issued. I am writing to ask that it do one thing no proclamation has
yet done: call on the head of every federal agency to hold a reading of the Constitution
aloud that day. Furthermore, that you, President Trump, the Vice President and the White
House executive office staff set the example by reading the Constitution aloud together
in the Oval Office, with press in attendance. Each cabinet secretary should do likewise
in his or her agency headquarters.

Since December 2004, federal law has required every agency head to give each employee
educational materials about the Constitution on that exact date — Public Law 108-447,
Division J, Title I, Section 111(a). The materials go out every year. In more than
twenty years, nobody has been asked to read the document aloud.

Five requests, in ascending order of effort:

1. Add one sentence to this year's proclamation naming agency heads. The proclamation
   has not been issued and can be amended up to the hour it is.
2. Set the example. Read it yourself, at the White House, on camera. An hour of your
   voice - together with the voices of your executive staff - reading the document you
   swore an oath to, would do more for civic education than a decade of handouts.
3. Issue a standing executive order making the reading recurring, so that it does not
   depend on any one President remembering. The model text is already drafted and
   released into the public domain — free to issue, amend, rename, or discard, with no
   permission needed and no credit owed:  
   https://github.com/jaredscribe/public-readings-of-the-founding-documents-act/blob/main/track-c-executive-order.md
4. Encourage the House to unilaterally adopt a chamber rule making it their practice to
   read the Constitution on the House floor, this and every future Sept. 17th. The House
   has authorized such a reading five times since 2011, never as a standing rule, and the
   Congress now sitting has not authorized it at all. A model resolution:  
   https://github.com/jaredscribe/public-readings-of-the-founding-documents-act/blob/main/track-b-chamber-rule-resolution.md
5. Encourage Congress to take up legislation requiring that schools, colleges, and
   universities receiving federal monies satisfy their existing Constitution Day
   obligation by reading the document aloud to assembled students, and that state library
   agencies carry the observance into public libraries. A model bill:  
   https://github.com/jaredscribe/public-readings-of-the-founding-documents-act/blob/main/track-a-public-readings-of-the-founding-documents-act.md

The nation marked its 250th birthday this July. The Constitution's own 250th falls on
September 17, 2037. A practice begun this Thursday would be well established by then.

Reading it aloud needs no appropriation, no legislation, no new office, and no new
employee. A microphone, willing readers, and an hour.

The petition making this request:  
https://www.change.org/p/read-the-constitution-aloud-on-constitution-day-every-federal-agency-every-year

I will be in Washington the week of September 17. If no agency holds a reading, I intend
to read the Constitution aloud on the Capitol steps myself. I would far rather report
that the government read it first.

Respectfully,

Jared Essig  
https://declareindependence.net
